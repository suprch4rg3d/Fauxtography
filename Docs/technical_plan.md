# Fauxtography: AI Image Detection

**Semester Group Project — MAI644 Computer Vision.**

## Technical Plan
We will build a classification system to detect whether an image is a real photograph or generated by AI. We will compare a baseline model against two powerful foundation models.

First we will gather a dataset containing real photographs and AI generated images. We will ensure the dataset has clear labels and is divided into training validation and testing sets.

Next we will develop our baseline model. We will take a standard convolutional neural network like ResNet and fine tune it on our training data. We will track the loss and accuracy during training to ensure the model learns the basic differences between real and fake images.

Then we will implement CLIP to test its zero shot capabilities. We will feed the test images into CLIP along with text prompts asking if the image is a real photo or an AI generation. We will record its predictions without doing any additional training.

Finally we will utilize DINOv2. Since DINOv2 is a vision model without a text component we will use it to extract deep visual features from our images. We will train a simple linear classifier on top of these extracted features to make the final prediction.

## Analysis Strategy
We will evaluate all three models using our test dataset. We will calculate the overall accuracy for the baseline ResNet model and CLIP and DINOv2.

We will generate a confusion matrix for each model. This will show us how often a real photo was flagged as fake and how often a fake photo slipped through as real.

We will perform a manual visual inspection of the errors. We will look at the specific images where the models failed. We will document the visual artifacts or specific image styles that confused the AI. This will form the core of our presentation and show our understanding of the methods.
